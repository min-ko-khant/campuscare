Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions, Clear Answers

Find useful information about CampusCare, university updates, events, activities, examination notices and campus services.

CampusCare is a campus information platform for Polytechnic University (Maubin). It brings together university news, announcements, events, student activities, examination notices and emergency updates in one place.

The latest university updates are available in the News section. Select “Read More” on any news item to view the full article and related details.

Visit the Events section to view upcoming workshops, seminars, competitions, sports programs, training sessions and volunteer activities. Each event page includes its date, time, location and full details.

Student activities are listed in the Activities section. Open an activity to view its description, date, images and available media.

Examination schedules and academic notices are published in the Exam Notices section. Students should check this section regularly for timetable changes and other important updates.

Important safety notices and urgent campus updates are displayed prominently on the homepage through the Emergency Notice section.

You can send questions or feedback through the Contact Form. You may also email minkokhant153@gmail.com or call 09696422314 .

Polytechnic University (Maubin) is located in Maubin Township, Ayeyarwady Region, Myanmar. The Campus Map section provides Google Maps access and directions to the university.

If you notice outdated or incorrect information, please send the details through the Contact Form. Reports will be reviewed so the information can be corrected as soon as possible.
